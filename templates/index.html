Our AI engine will automatically categorize, prioritize, and route your complaint.

How it works

  1. Describe your issue in your own words — no need to pick a category.
  2. AI analyzes it instantly: category, urgency, and sentiment.
  3. Safety-critical issues (harassment, ragging, injury) are flagged as Critical and escalated immediately.
  4. Duplicate detection merges repeated complaints so common issues get fixed faster.
  5. Track your complaint's status: Submitted → Acknowledged → In Progress → Resolved.
